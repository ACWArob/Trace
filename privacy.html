Your life, kept with care.

Privacy information for the invite-only Trace beta. Updated November 13, 2026.

Your device and your account

A device shelf stays on that device. Signing in creates a separate private account library. Copying a device shelf into an account is an explicit action. Your account library can include ratings, notes, dates, imported history, memory photos, and attachments.

Account libraries are stored on the Trace server and cached on signed-in devices for offline use. Passwords, invitation tokens, session tokens, and recovery codes are stored as hashes where verification allows it. Recovery codes are shown once; save them somewhere private.

Your account library is private from other members. It is not end-to-end encrypted. The server operator can access stored data and backups to run the service. Hosted connections use HTTPS.

What you choose to share

Publishing a review makes the selected title, review, rating, profile, and interactions visible through this server’s social features. Other people may copy public posts. Saving a title or importing a collection does not publish it automatically.

Catalogs and outside services

Live searches send the requested title, author, artist, ISBN, or public profile identifier to the relevant catalog or profile provider. Catalog metadata and public scores may be cached and reused across members. Provider API keys stay on the server.

Cover images, external review links, and web fonts may load from third-party services. Those services receive ordinary network information such as an IP address. Their own privacy terms apply when you use them.

Export, deletion, and backups

Export your collection from Connections. Account settings lets you replace recovery codes or permanently delete your account after confirming your password and username. Deletion removes the active account, synced library, photos, reviews, and associated interactions from this server.

Separate device shelves and previously exported files remain under their owners’ control. Other offline devices can retain cached copies until their local data is cleared. A server backup made before deletion can retain older data until the operator retires it. Recovery from such a backup must reapply account deletions before reopening the service.

Beta support

For a privacy request, backup question, or help deleting data, contact the person who invited you to this Trace server. Avoid sending passwords, recovery codes, or private exports in a bug report.
