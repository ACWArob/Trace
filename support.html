A little help.

Trace is an invite-only beta. Contact the person who invited you for an invitation or support.

Keep your shelf with you

Use the same hosted Trace address on each device, sign in, and wait for “Synced with your account.” Your separate device shelf can be copied from Connections after you review the merge.

Install on your phone

On Android, open the hosted address in Chrome and choose Install app. On iPhone, open it in Safari, use Share, then Add to Home Screen. Native Android and TestFlight builds must come from your beta host.

Lost your password?

Choose “Forgot your password?” on the sign-in screen. Enter your username, one saved recovery code, and a new password. This signs out old sessions and replaces your recovery codes. Save the new set. If you have no usable code, contact your beta host; do not send them your password.

Connection or sync trouble

Keep the app’s data in place while changes are pending. Reconnect and use Sync now. Review any conflict before choosing a version. Export a backup from Connections before reinstalling or clearing app data.

Report a problem

Include your phone model, Android or iOS version, the action that failed, and the time. A screenshot helps, after you hide private notes and photos. Never include passwords, recovery codes, invitation codes, or a collection export.

Leave the beta

Export anything you want to keep, then use Connections → Account settings & recovery → Delete account. Separate device shelves stay on their devices. See privacy information for offline copies and backups.
